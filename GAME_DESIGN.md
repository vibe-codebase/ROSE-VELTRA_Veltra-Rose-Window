# Veltra Rose Window: design notes

**Fantasy:** a glazier restoring a cathedral window, coaxing the light where it belongs.

**Rules:** light travels cell to cell. Plain mirrors turn when tapped, gilded mirrors are fixed, stone stops light, and coloured glass adds its pigment (all three make umber, which goes dark). Half-silvered glass both passes and reflects. A medallion glows when light of exactly its colour reaches it, and the window is finished when every medallion glows at once.

**Fairness:** every board is checked by brute force over all mirror arrangements. Par is the true minimum, and the tests fail if a level's stated par is wrong or if it starts already solved. No board has more than four different shortest answers.

**Feel:** a soft glassy click on every turn, a handbell when a medallion lights, motes of dust drifting along the beams to show their direction, and a choir swell with coloured light pooling on the floor when the window is finished.
